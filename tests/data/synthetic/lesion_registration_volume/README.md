# LesionTOADS registration volume accumulation fixture

`java_reference.json` is generated by
`tests/java_reference/LesionRegistrationVolumeAccumulationReferenceHarness.java`
using `scripts/generate_java_lesion_registration_volume_reference.sh`. It is a
standalone float32 transcription of the selected
`LesionToadAtlas.computeScaledRegistrationCoefficients` loop and its
`registrationCost`, `registrationNorm`, `registrationCostGradient`, and
`registrationCostHessian` terms.

The deterministic pre-sampled 4×3×5 field has four classes, nonzero class-0
priors, multiple positive registered-class priors, patterned zero priors, and
voxel-varying memberships, spatial derivatives, and rigid Jacobians. The
fixture records a dense source-order scan, an offset-zero step-two scan, and a
reverse-order control. Dense and reverse accumulations differ at float32
precision, demonstrating why the voxel-major/class-major reduction order is
part of this acceptance test.

This fixture validates reduction and final norm scaling only. Priors,
derivatives, and Jacobians are pre-sampled; it does not validate atlas
interpolation, pyramid setup, the line search, transform optimization, or
LesionTOADS segmentation.
