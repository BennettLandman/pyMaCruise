# MaCRUISEplus nested surface phantom

Three 17³ synthetic RAS level sets form concentric physical spheres with
radii 3, 4, and 5 mm on an anisotropic 1.0 × 1.5 × 2.0 mm grid. They validate
the plus adapter that routes uncropped level sets through the production
source-derived JIST connectivity-18 case templates. Meshes are expected in
RAS+ millimetres and to be closed. Saved workflow level sets have separate
direct selected-JIST comparisons in
`docs/reports/jist-connectivity18-case-template-port-2026-10-08.md`.

Regenerate the compressed NIfTI level sets with `make_phantom.py`.
