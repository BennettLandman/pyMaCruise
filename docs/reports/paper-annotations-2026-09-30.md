# Paper-to-code annotation progress

This report records verified paper concepts and where they are connected to code. The comments distinguish published method descriptions from the selected VandyCRUISE behavior, external-library behavior, and bounded synthetic validation. They do not claim that a cited paper validates every implementation detail or the complete workflows.
