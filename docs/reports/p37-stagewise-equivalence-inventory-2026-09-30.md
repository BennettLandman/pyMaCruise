# P37 Stage-wise Equivalence Evidence Inventory — 2026-09-30

## Status

This is an inventory of existing synthetic Java/source comparison evidence, not a P37 pass. P35 (synthetic readiness), P36 (canonical reference capture), and P37 (stage-wise equivalence) are all **Not started** in [`plan.md`](../../plan.md#L217). P08's policy is defined, but its acceptance criterion that every output class have a method or documented exception is not yet demonstrated for complete workflows. P37 requires stage-wise comparison to MATLAB/JIST or trusted references, deterministic label equality where expected, agreed P08 metrics for numerical/final products, transform-direction and mesh-coordinate checks, and explicit decisions for deviations. This report did not run tests or recompute metrics; figures below are transcribed from cited reports and machine-readable result files.

The inventory includes focused P18cyb/cyc/cyd method-reference evidence, the P18cye alternate-constructor membership method slice, recent P39 Java-reference compositions, and the new adversarial surface comparison. These updates do not replace the historical full-suite snapshot, establish a current full-suite result, or close P35–P37. Source tracing shows that the selected `MedicAlgorithmToads08` wrapper uses the shorter constructor, which forces `useApprox=false`; P18cye's `approx=4` implementation exercises a separate alternate-constructor path, not the selected wrapper's active membership dispatch. It therefore must not be cited as validating selected-wrapper TOADS membership or used to claim whole-workflow completeness.

The owner-approved thresholds in [`validation-tolerances.md`](../validation-tolerances.md) are:

- **Each required segmentation label/region:** Dice at least 0.97.
- **Surface distance:** symmetric Hausdorff at most 1 mm RAS+.
- **Surface position:** symmetric mean nearest-surface distance at most 0.01 mm RAS+, with mesh vertices as deterministic samples and exact closest points on the opposing triangles.

For deterministic label maps, exact voxel equality on a fixture implies Dice 1 for that compared map. It does not establish an untested class/region, larger geometry, end-stage output, or complete workflow.

### Bounded results that meet measured thresholds

The ACE/ThinACE phantom outputs, the reported 25³ TGDM stage masks and sampled surfaces, and the folded Dura TGDM final mask meet the numerical P08 thresholds **for their specific compared products and fixtures**, as detailed below. The direct JIST/Lewiner level-set comparisons also have very small sampled distances, but those vertex-sampled maxima are not continuous Hausdorff bounds. None of these bounded passes is a P08 pass for all output classes: there is no whole-workflow, per-required-label Dice and surface-metric report for TOADS, LesionTOADS, MaCRUISE, or MaCRUISEplus.

## Metrics absent or not yet decision-ready

1. **No end-to-end base or plus comparison.** P34 is Blocked, P39 is Started, and P40/P41 are not complete. The current evidence therefore cannot supply a per-required-label Dice table and surface metrics for all promised base/plus products. See the status summaries in [`plan.md`](../../plan.md#L17), P34/P39–P41 in [`plan.md`](../../plan.md#L216), and P39's status overview in [`plan.md`](../../plan.md#L26).
2. **Most TOADS/LesionTOADS rows are component contracts.** This remains true for P18cyb/cyc/cyd's new exact field/centroid/variance references and P39zzzzzzag/ah's one-voxel/volume membership comparisons. Their oracles compare intermediate numerical state, not P08 Dice and surface metrics for final anatomical output labels. Explicitly retain this distinction even where the numerical comparison is exact.
3. **The larger folded TGDM Java match is only partial.** P29's selected 25³ Java comparison passes all three reported thresholds; the 41³ folded Java comparison is not present. The separate 25³ folded Dura TGDM P18cj result does pass, but covers that one Dura evolution slice and its final mask.
4. **Surface extraction has a passing sampled metric but a material mesh substitution.** The P57 fixture's measured vertex-to-triangle values are tiny, but face connectivity differs materially. The sample maximum is not a continuous Hausdorff certificate, and the observed thickness delta has no P08-wide threshold. Keep this risk open under P37 pending broader level sets and downstream mesh-dependent comparisons.
5. **Scalar-volume thresholds remain stage-specific.** P08 defines no universal scalar MAE/RMSE/max-error bound. ACE/TGDM/TGDM-distance and lesion energy deviations must keep their source-specific evidence and acceptance decisions, rather than being silently absorbed into Dice/surface thresholds.
6. **Transform and physical-grid substitutions need explicit P37 disposition.** ANTsPy registration substitutes for Aladin/NiftyReg; check direction, landmarks, interpolation, target grid, and all surfaces/meshes transferred through the transforms. P33/P38 evidence validates declared synthetic contracts, not optimizer identity with those unavailable tools.
7. **Evidence snapshot is mixed in freshness.** The latest plan-level full-suite figure is 810 passed on `d84f2039fe45e6c90cd040a559cc09029a379b7a`, and `plan.md` notes newer P18/P39 code is not included. Focused Java-reference passes recorded for recent slices are not a full-suite result. P18cye's alternate-constructor method implementation exists, but its relationship to the selected wrapper is separate: the selected short constructor forces `useApprox=false`. Rerun the complete suite after the current source changes are integrated before attributing current pass status to this inventory.
